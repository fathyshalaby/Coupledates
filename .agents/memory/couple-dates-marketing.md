---
name: Couple Dates marketing direction
description: Current public brand, audience, and publication boundaries for marketing.
---

Couple Dates is the public brand. The lead customer is the girlfriend who posts often and wants to plan dates with her partner and create memories worth sharing. Lead with that initiator while making clear both partners choose the date and memories stay private unless selected for sharing. Long-distance couples are also supported.

Keep draft Privacy and Terms pages out of public navigation until accurate business contact details are supplied. Do not invent testimonials, contact details, policies, app-store badges, or product capabilities.

**Why:** The user identified the girlfriend who posts often as the main person using the product, with date planning and share-worthy memories as her goals.

**How to apply:** Use Couple Dates in public titles, navigation, and copy. Address the initiating partner directly, preserve the other partner’s say in plans, make sharing explicitly opt-in, and keep claims aligned to features that exist.
