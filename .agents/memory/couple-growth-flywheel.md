---
name: Couple growth flywheel
description: Product direction connecting private partner acquisition with healthy couple engagement.
---

The user wants invitation-driven growth and product vitality treated as one couple-growth flywheel: one partner invites the other, then both move through ideas, agreement, shared dates, memories, and the next idea.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose both virality and product vitality as one connected growth engine.

**How to apply:** Keep growth private and relationship-centered. Prefer the existing partner-invite loop and meaningful shared actions; do not add public feeds, competitive scoring, or guilt-based streak mechanics without a direct request.
