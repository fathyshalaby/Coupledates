---
name: Visual refinement intent
description: Why the existing Couple Dates interface was made more restrained instead of replaced.
---

The user asked for Couple Dates to look “less vibe coded” and wanted improvements to its design and UX.

**Why:** The request was to mature an existing working couples product, not replace it with another template or change its features.

**How to apply:** Keep future additions consistent with the refined shared interface. Prioritize meaningful actions and real couple content over decorative treatments or empty widgets that compete with the page's purpose. Preserve working flows when refining their presentation.
